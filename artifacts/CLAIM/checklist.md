# CLAIM — Review Checklist

- [ ] Statement readable by a domain expert without opening any reference?
- [ ] Every quantifier explicit (over what models, what alphabets, what asymptotics)?
- [ ] Exact Data internally consistent (e.g. probabilities normalize — verify by exact summation; constraints well-formed)?
- [ ] Model Class definition matches the cited literature exactly (for probabilistic models: the decomposition formula and the source/party labeling conventions)?
- [ ] Finite-dimensionality of the check either established (with citation) or its absence acknowledged?
- [ ] Provenance complete: original conjecture, proof paper, arXiv IDs resolve (run reference-audit)?
- [ ] Claim minimal: would splitting it produce independently checkable parts?
- [ ] Status field consistent with the Verification section?
